Poplur2025 archive/export fixes applied:

1. Removed WeatherKit entitlement from Poplur2025.entitlements.
   This often causes export/upload failure unless the App ID/provisioning profile has WeatherKit enabled.

2. Removed PNG alpha channels from all AppIcon PNGs.
   App Store Connect commonly rejects 1024x1024 icons that contain an alpha channel, even if fully opaque.

After opening in Xcode:
- Product > Clean Build Folder
- Xcode > Settings > Accounts > Download Manual Profiles
- Target Poplur2025 > Signing & Capabilities: verify your Team and Bundle ID.
- Archive again, then Distribute App.

If it still fails, copy the first red error from Organizer/Distribution Logs.
